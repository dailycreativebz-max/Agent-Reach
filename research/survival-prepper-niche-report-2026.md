## PART 2 — THE DEMAND DATA (every hard number that matters)

### 2.1 Population & behavior
- **24.5M** self-identified US preppers (**9.1%** of adults) — TruePrepper synthesis of FEMA National Household Survey data; up ~2× since 2017
- **81.7M** adults = the FEMA ceiling (30.3% "continuously preparing + high preparedness efficacy")
- **83%** of US adults took **3+ preparedness actions** in 2024 (FEMA), up from 57% the prior year — the casual/mainstream audience is enormous
- **69%** have assembled emergency supplies, but only **12.5%** can last over a month — a giant skill/content gap between interest and readiness
- Only **5%** of US households have a complete emergency kit; only **26%** feel confident they'd know what to do in a major disaster (SafeHome 2025 study)
- Demographics are broadening fast: more **women, younger, urban, and progressive** preppers; Black women preppers are a fast-growing creator/audience segment (Oregon Capital Chronicle, 2025)

### 2.2 The 2026 defining anxiety: THE GRID
This is the single biggest demand engine in the niche right now:
- **NERC Level 3 alert (May 4, 2026)** — the top North American grid watchdog's *highest-urgency* alert ever for data centers: AI/computational loads swinging **1,000+ MW within seconds**, utilities ordered to file mitigation plans by Aug 3
- **Forced-outage rate hit 9.2%** (NERC 2026 State of Reliability) vs. 7–8% historical norm
- **July 2026:** DOE emergency order — PJM (13 states + DC, ~160M people) authorized to force data centers onto their own backup generation during the heat dome; DOE estimates **35 GW of unused backup generation** nationwide (≈26M homes)
- **+224 GW** projected summer peak-demand growth over the next decade
- **Sunrun/Talker survey (Aug 18–24, 2026, n=2,000 homeowners):** 66% experienced a power outage in 2026; 74% of those fear another within a year; 59% believe outages are becoming more common; **75% worry data centers will cause future outages**; 47% had a "power-outage reality check"; **41% own NO backup power of any kind**; only 19% have a backup power source; 28% have an electricity-dependent medical need in the household (insulin refrigeration 39%, CPAP 36%, oxygen 30%)
- **Spain/Portugal, April 28, 2025:** a 10–16 hour nationwide blackout in a modern Western country — payments down, comms down, 8 deaths (including carbon-monoxide deaths from a badly-placed generator, and a candle fire). ENTSO-E final report published March 20, 2026 kept the story alive. This is the "it can happen here" proof point every video can cite.
- **Hurricane Melissa (Cat 5, Oct 28, 2025):** strongest storm ever recorded hitting Jamaica; a full-hemisphere reminder every October.

### 2.3 The food/inflation engine
- Beef +11.8% YoY (cattle herd smallest in 75 years), coffee +19%, fresh vegetables +9.9%, gasoline +34% since Jan 2025; groceries +3.4% since Jan 2025; ~$540/yr added tariffs cost per household; 62% of Americans "extremely concerned" about prices
- **84% of US consumers** did at least one home food-production activity (Curion, n=15,000+, July 2026); **over a third** now preserve food by canning/freezing/fermenting
- **#canning has 69.5M TikTok posts**; "prepper pantry" / pantry-restock TikToks routinely pull 400K–1.6M likes; #preppertok / #prepperpantry / #shtfprepping2026 are active mega-tags
- Home freeze-dryer market: $500M (2024) → $1.2B by 2033 (10.5% CAGR); "home freeze dryer machine" appeared as a *new* Google query mid-2025 and peaked Sept 2025 — an emerging, unsaturated search term
- Portable power station market: **$5.4B (2026) → $23.2B (2035)**; EcoFlow did ~$580M revenue in 2025; Jackery has sold 6M+ units — backup power has gone fully mainstream

### 2.4 The political/uncertainty engine
- **Gallup (Apr–Jun 2026, n=23,686): 73% of US adults** report significant concern about multiple aspects of the Nov 3, 2026 midterm election process; 80% worry leaders will take possibly-illegal actions

### 5.2 Topic gaps (high-demand, low-supply video topics)
1. **Blackout-specific practical content** — what to do in hour 1 of an outage; fridge/food rules; CPAP/insulin power plans; generator vs. power station decision; "blackout box" builds. Demand: all-time high (Part 2.2). Supply: generic "emergency kit" videos from 2019.
2. **AI + grid explainers** — the NERC/data-center story translated into household action. Demand: mainstream news cycle. Supply in niche: zero credible.
3. **Budget stock-up systems** — "$20/week pantry building," "$200 blackout box," "prepping on food stamps." Supply: near zero.
4. **Food-preservation restart** — canning basics, vacuum sealing, freeze-dryer payback math (ties to emerging search demand).
5. **Medical preparedness** — Rx stockpiling legally, building a real trauma kit, insulin/CPAP power.
6. **Prepping documentation & money prep** — insurance inventory, cash strategy, documents grab-file. Nobody makes these; they're shareable to a mainstream audience.
7. **Communications plans** — family comms tree, radio basics (GMRS/FRS), offline maps. Underserved vs. its importance.
8. **Sanitation** — the single most-ignored topic in the niche (everyone does food, nobody does greywater/toilets). Differentiator.
9. **Scenario storytelling** — "Hour by hour: the first 72 hours of a blackout" narrative format (the Sept 2026 "30 Days Without Power" video proves the format).
10. **Doom-recovery content** — "You don't need a bunker," anti-fear preparedness. Directly answers the documented 2026 doom-fatigue.

- **Series/playlist engineering** — incumbents upload one-offs; session-contribution structure is a free algorithm advantage
- **Lead magnets** (free PDF checklists) — standard in every other niche, unused here → email list nobody in the niche has

### 5.4 Competitor blind spots (your unfair advantages)

2. Incumbents ignore analytics-driven packaging (titles/thumbnails A/B testing, CTR benchmarks)

6. Their formats age (talking head + news scroll); mobile-first, fast-cut, chart-driven formats win 2026 retention curves

## PART 10 — RISKS, TRAPS & WHAT NOT TO DO (the honest section)

1. **Don't build a doom channel.** The fast-growth path in this niche is fear escalation — a documented misinformation channel went 6K→91K subs in a year and got fact-checked for fabricated "emergency" claims. It's also the fast-death path: demonetization, fact-checks, audience churn, and the documented 2026 doom-fatigue. Every script above converts fear into a checklist — keep it that way.
2. **Advertiser-safety boundaries:** avoid graphic crisis imagery, keep firearms out of the first 30 seconds and thumbnails (yellow-icon risk), avoid naming live political figures, and always attach actionable, official guidance (FEMA/Ready.gov/NOAA/NERC) to any threat claim. This niche sits one clickbait away from limited ads.
3. **Never fabricate or overstate threat claims** — cite sources on screen (every script above embeds real, verifiable 2025–26 data). Credibility IS the moat: incumbents burned theirs.
4. **Don't title for preppers.** Title for outcomes ("keep food cold without power," "one bin"). Identity terms are declining; outcome queries are rising (Part 3).
5. **Don't rely on search alone.** V4/V2 are search assets, but search alone grows in lines, not spikes. The system above deliberately mixes one news-cycle video (V1), one entertainment-format video (V2), one time-boxed spike video (V3), and two evergreen assets (V4/V5).
6. **Seasonality is real:** June–August is the trough (except hurricane regions). Use it to bank evergreen content for the Sept–Jan harvest, when Q4 CPMs are also highest.
7. **Shorts RPM is pennies ($0.02–0.12/1K).** Shorts are a funnel, not a business — never restructure long-form for Shorts money.
8. **YPP timing:** you need 1K subs + 4K watch-hours (or 10M Shorts views). At the cadence above, expect monetization in months 2–4. Affiliate income starts on video one — put links in descriptions from day 1 (disclosed).
9. **The misinformation trap on the grid story:** NERC's alert is about *reliability risk*, not a prediction of imminent collapse. Every script says "risk is rising; here's what covers you" — never "the grid WILL collapse on X date." That line is the difference between a channel and a lawsuit.
10. **The one unresearched variable:** you. On-camera presence or a distinctive voice/format (data-graphics style works faceless) is the multiplier no research can supply. Pick the lane whose 50 future video ideas you could actually enjoy making — competence reads on camera, and the niche's audience can smell faked authority.
